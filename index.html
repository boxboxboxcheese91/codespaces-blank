'I'iwi

The 'I'iwi or Scarlet Honeycreeper is a species of Hawaiian honeycreeper and is a highly recognized symbol of hawaii.

Diet

The 'I'iwi primarily feeds on nectar from native Hawaiian plants, particularly those with tubular flowers. They also eat small anthropods.

Habitat & Distribution

The largest populations of the 'I'iwi are in the Big Island followed by Maui with most in maui being in East Maui. They are altitudinal migrants, following the progress of flowers and develop at increasing altiudes throughout the year.

Locations where they still exist

  1. Big Island
  2. Maui
  3. Kauai - Less than 1%
Where there are potentially remnants or don't exist
  • Oahu
  • Moloka'i
  • Lana'i - no longer present
